# IQ3 and official checkpoint provenance

The preserved IQ3 GGUF is SHA256 `58fd826723939933dc86f45b7fe04545cbc2de1c70f6fe2cdd3858c87a98c12f`, from [ISTA-DASLab's release pinned at c7b6817](https://huggingface.co/ISTA-DASLab/Qwen3.8-27B-GSQ-RCO-GGUF/blob/c7b6817b8affebf3b38c58313394b061345a4924/README.md). The publisher identifies Qwen3.8-27B as its base and describes per-tensor GSQ optimization followed by RCO selection and assembly, using an importance matrix. GSQ learns quantization assignments and scales; the resulting weights are not merely a uniform round-to-nearest or identical original weights. The publisher says the MTP variant adds the MTP head without changing the other weights. These are release-author descriptions, not independently verified build inputs or a guarantee about page-generation quality.

The local GGUF metadata identifies the base model as Qwen3.8 27B and Qwen as its organization but records no exact upstream revision. The official comparison stages `Qwen/Qwen3.8-27B` revision `1d4bf0f2ff6012fd82039f2fa52739d0dd7c60c0`. Matching vocabulary and selected architecture metadata support compatibility, but they do not prove that revision was ISTA's precise source. The inspected release description does not document an additional merged model, but absence of such a statement is not proof that no merge or other preprocessing occurred. Its MTP/non-MTP identity claim also remains a publisher claim here; the other full artifact was not checked.

A provisional lightweight signature supports common lineage: a 10,240-byte BF16 layer-0 input norm vector read from the already-downloading official shard's local partial file exactly matches `blk.0.attn_norm.weight` in the IQ3 GGUF after the required HF-to-GGUF `+1` normalization offset (5,120/5,120 float32 values; max absolute difference 0). The incomplete shard's header matches the independently retrieved pinned-revision header SHA256 `7bdeb03df566aa804dca2cb48e420908cb1864e5222858ad04918299883287e0`, but its full-file hash has not been verified. This is one retained F32 tensor and does not establish the exact source revision or every trunk weight. Do not substitute it for the eventual whole-shard checksum.

Once the official files complete and pass their pinned full-file hashes, compare several retained unquantized IQ3 tensors against correctly converted official counterparts from different layers: input/post-attention norms (`+1`), SSM norm, transformed `A_log` and reordered convolution vectors, recording every transform and exact equality. Check the released per-tensor allocation and MTP metadata separately. If signatures match they strengthen lineage evidence but still cannot prove every quantized tensor was derived from the same revision. Quantized matrices require separate dequantized error statistics, not exact-byte matching.

A discrepancy between IQ3 and official logits may reflect GSQ calibration/optimization and mixed per-tensor allocation, source revision or preprocessing, conversion/tensor ordering, backend arithmetic and graph differences. A comparison does not isolate bit width or identify the cause of the generated page's failure without further controlled evidence. The independent F32 Transformers-versus-converted-llama comparison remains necessary.

## Follow-up after official checkpoint verification

The official checkpoint subsequently completed and its files passed the pinned manifest verification. The lightweight `iq3_lineage.py` check compared retained F32 IQ3 tensors to transformed official tensors: **27 of 27 matched exactly** across layers 0, 20, 42, 60 and 63 plus output norm. The sample covers input/post-attention norms, SSM norm, reordered delta-time bias, reordered and transformed `A_log`, and reordered convolution tensors. The detailed results and verification-manifest hash are in `iq3-lineage-signatures.json`. This is stronger common-lineage evidence, but it still does not prove the exact source revision or identity of all quantized tensors.
